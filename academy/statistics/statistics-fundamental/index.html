Statistics Fundamentals

The statistics every data scientist needs: describing data, probability, distributions, inference, regression, experiments and the pitfalls that lead to wrong conclusions.

  • LevelBeginner
  • Modules8
  • ToolsPython: pandas, SciPy, statsmodels

Tutorial

Work through the course module by module. Use the notes and slides alongside the video.

What you'll learn

  1. 01

    Why statistics matters

    Turning data into decisions while being honest about uncertainty, and the mistakes that cost the most.

  2. 02

    Descriptive statistics

    Center, spread, shape and relationships, and why you always plot before you summarize.

  3. 03

    Probability foundations

    Random variables, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, expectation and the Central Limit Theorem.

  4. 04

    Key distributions

    Normal, binomial, Poisson, exponential, t, chi-square, F and heavy-tailed distributions, and when each applies.

  5. 05

    Inference and hypothesis testing

    Sampling, confidence intervals, p-values, error types and power, common tests, multiple comparisons and the bootstrap.

  6. 06

    Regression and modeling

    Linear and logistic regression, GLMs, time series basics and the bias-variance trade-off.

  7. 07

    Experimentation and causal inference

    A/B testing done right, and causal methods for when you can't run an experiment.

  8. 08

    Pitfalls and best practices

    Practical vs statistical significance, leakage and bias, outliers, small samples and pre-registration.

Prerequisites

  • School-level algebra
  • Basic Python (variables, lists, functions) for the exercises
  • No prior statistics needed

Who it's for

Aspiring data scientists, analysts, and engineers moving into machine learning.
