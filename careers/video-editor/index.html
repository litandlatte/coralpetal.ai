Video Editor

Enterprise content, AI-assisted workflows.

  • TeamContent
  • LocationFully remote
  • Must haveFigma, AI agents
  • Applycareers@coralpetal.ai

About the role

The Coral Petal Company helps enterprises adopt the latest advances in AI, end to end. We're looking for a video editor to turn raw recordings into polished, enterprise-grade video for courses, product walkthroughs, client presentations and our YouTube channel. You'll work with AI agents as part of your everyday workflow.

What you'll do

  • Edit long-form and short-form video to a consistent brand standard: pacing, cuts, color, audio cleanup, captions, lower thirds and motion graphics.
  • Turn screen recordings, talking-head footage and slide decks into clear, structured learning content.
  • Work with AI agents for transcription, rough cuts, B-roll search, subtitles and translations, and review and correct what they produce.
  • Write clear briefs and prompts for agents, check their output for accuracy and brand fit, and keep a human sign-off on everything that ships.
  • Build reusable templates, presets and checklists so every video meets the same quality bar.
  • Manage files, versions and delivery formats for web, LinkedIn, YouTube and enterprise learning platforms.

What you bring

  • A strong portfolio of professional editing, ideally including corporate, training or technical content.
  • Expert use of at least one of Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro; After Effects or similar for motion graphics.
  • Strong Figma skills: building and editing slides, thumbnails, title cards and on-screen graphics, and working from a shared design system.
  • Hands-on experience with AI video and audio tools, and with working alongside AI agents: delegating tasks, reviewing their work and knowing where human judgment matters.
  • A sharp eye for detail, accessibility (captions, contrast, readable text) and a consistent brand.
  • Clear written communication and comfort working remotely and asynchronously.

Nice to have

  • Familiarity with data science, AI or cloud topics.
  • Experience with YouTube optimization.

How to apply

Send your CV, a portfolio link and a short note on how you've worked with AI agents in your editing.
